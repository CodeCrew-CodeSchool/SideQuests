## Growth Mindset

Having a Growth Mindset is one of the most important things you can have in life.

To me, Growth Mindset is all about you having the mindset to be better and willing to fight and grind for your goals and dreams through adversity.

This ariticle [Growth Mindset](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/inside-atlassian/growth-mindset) showed me that growing and being successful doesn't just start with action - you must first think before you do.

3 Thoughts I gathered from the Growth Mindset Article that I will remember:

1. Embraces the struggles and challenges (Pressure makes Diamonds!)
2. Failure is great! You learn from your mistakes
3. Don't be envious and jealous of people who are successful. Learn from them!
