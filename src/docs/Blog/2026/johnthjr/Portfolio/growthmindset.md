#***A Growth Mindset***

**To me, a growth mindset is the belief that any skill, no matter any previous experience or aptitudes, can be improved through perseverance and hard work.**

**3 Reminders of How to Keep A Growth Mindset**
    1.  Surround yourself with like-minded, motivated individuals
    2.  Don't take yourself so seriously
    3.  Have a good sense of humor
