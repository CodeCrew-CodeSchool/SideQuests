# Growth Mindset

**Growth mindset** = believing that your ability isn't limited, and that you can improve through **consistency**, learning from mistakes, **communication**, and **getting help** when you need it.

### What I took from the article

Success of others: especially in a coding class, somebody else understanding something faster doesn't mean you're behind. You can learn from them, and when you understand something they don't, you can help them too.

---

## Reminders to myself to keep myself in a growth mindset

- Stay consistent, even on days when I don't feel like it.
- Ask for help when I need it instead of staying stuck.
- Communicate with my team and make sure everyone is moving forward.
