## Growth Mindset

---------------------

/What is Growth Mindset?:

/Growth Mindset is the ability to overcome challenges, accept and improve on criticism, and acknowledge that without effort there will be no results. I believe that last part is the most important and one that people struggle with the most. That is because people like good stuff fast as that gives an instant "satisfaction" and who doesn't but when it comes to those tasks we see dreadful those are the ones that will give you and even greater satisfaction while on the journey rather than on the result because even when we complete tasks or problems after they are done, that gratification goes away fast and we are left with a sense of *empty*
