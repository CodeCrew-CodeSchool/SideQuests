# Growth Mindset


A growth mindset will presevre through hardships and challenges in order to become a stronger, more opened minded person. A growth mindset will accept rather than run from challenges, ask questions when it doesn't understand, and help others who are also stuggling.


A growth mindset also knows when to step back and reflect when difficulties become to much to bare, placing ego aside to ask for help, and seeing the situation from another point of view. Taking critism with stride rather than shame, to learn from one's own mistakes to grow into a person that can and will take every obstacle with excitement for another opportunity to be better.
